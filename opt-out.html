California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) & California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) Notice

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific statutory rights regarding their personal data, including:

  • Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing: The right to direct a business not to sell or share their personal information to third parties.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information (SPI): The right to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information (including precise geolocation) to necessary operational purposes.
No Server-Side Personal Data Storage:
Springwalk does not collect, host, or store any personal identifiers, user account data, or location logs on our servers. All media conversion and file processing take place entirely on your device.

However, to support free application services, our mobile apps may include third-party SDK partners (such as Outlogic and ad networks) that collect device identifiers and precise location data (Wi-Fi SSID/BSSID, Bluetooth scan records, and GPS) for personalized advertising, location-based analytics, and research.

1. Opt Out & Limit Use Within the Mobile App (Recommended)

You can immediately exercise your rights to opt out of data sharing and limit the use of sensitive personal information directly within the app:

  1. Open the MediaConverter (or MP3 Video Converter) app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (or Information / Menu).
  3. Select Privacy / Consent Settings (CMP Consent).
  4. Tap either:
    • "Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information" (immediately restricts precise location and sensitive data collection), OR
    • "Do Not Sell My Info" (opts out of third-party data sale/sharing).

Selecting either option immediately halts data collection by third-party partner SDKs (including Outlogic) for that app installation.

3. Device-Level Privacy Controls

You can also manage or restrict ad tracking and location access at the operating system level:

  • Android Advertising ID: Open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and select "Delete advertising ID" or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • App Location Permissions: Open Settings > Apps > MediaConverter > Permissions > Location and change permission to "Don't allow".
